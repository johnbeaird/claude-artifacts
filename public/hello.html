Hello,
claudeshare.

A working surface for fast-moving artifacts — briefings drafted in motion, demos built long enough to settle a question, tools assembled and shipped before the meeting that called for them.

This space is the front door to a quiet pipeline: Claude drafts an HTML artifact, it lands in a GitHub folder, Cloudflare deploys it, and it shows up here — usually within the minute. No build step, no framework, no ceremony.

The point isn't to host a website. The point is to remove the friction between thinking out loud with an AI and having something I can hand someone — a client, a colleague, a future me. Most of what gets shipped here has a half-life measured in days. That's a feature.

If you've landed on a specific artifact and don't have context, the page itself is the answer. If you're poking around the index because someone shared a link, welcome — most of the work here is field-notes-shaped: a thesis, a sketch, a take, occasionally a tool.

✦

Every artifact follows the same loose grammar: a clear <title> so the indexer can find it, a single page that does one thing, and no dependencies it can't fetch from a CDN. If you're forking one for your own use, that's the spec.

Things you might find here over time: security briefings, positioning decks for client conversations, readiness assessments, RAMR-MC dashboards when the trading system has something to show, and the occasional field guide from the road. Some of it is internal. Most of it isn't sensitive. Where it is, you won't see a link.
